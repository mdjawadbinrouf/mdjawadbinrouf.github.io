Since June 2025 I have been a Lecturer in the Department of Industrial and Production Engineering at Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST), Dhaka.

#### Teaching

- Teach undergraduate courses across the industrial and production engineering curriculum: lesson planning, assignments, assessment and progress tracking.
- Contribute to curriculum development aligned with Outcome-Based Education (OBE).
- Take part in laboratory improvement initiatives in the department.

#### Academic service

- **Faculty Advisor**, IEOM Society AUST Student Chapter (September 2026 to present). Mentoring the student executive committee in organizing conferences, workshops and competitions.
- **Member, Thesis Committee** (November 2025 to present). Coordinating undergraduate thesis supervisor selection and organizing pre-defense and defense sessions.
- **Member, AUST IPE Society** (July 2025 to present). Running student leadership elections and supporting departmental programs and events.
- **Member, Industrial Attachment and Farewell Committee** (June 2025 to November 2025). Coordinated industrial attachments with industry partners.
- **Department Fire Warden** (November 2025 to present). Overseeing departmental fire safety, evacuation procedures and drills.
