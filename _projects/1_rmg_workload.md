I worked as a research assistant in the Department of Industrial and Production Engineering, AUST (April 2024 to June 2025), on the project **Enhancing Occupational Safety and Ergonomics in Bangladesh's Ready-Made Garments (RMG) Industry: Multisensory Workload Analysis with Machine Learning**. Principal Investigator: [Mr. Shah Murtoza Morshed](https://www.aust.edu/mpe/faculty_member/mr_shah_murtoza_morshed).

#### What I did

- Visited RMG factories, coordinated with industry stakeholders, and collected real-time workload and ergonomic data from workers.
- Set up and maintained the multisensory acquisition system (biosignalsplux with OpenSignals) recording **ECG, EMG, EDA and EEG** during garment-packaging tasks.
- Preprocessed the signals and extracted features to assess workers' physiological workload.
- Contributed to machine learning model development and hyperparameter tuning for workload classification, evaluating how well models generalize to new workers with **leave-one-subject-out cross-validation**.
- Managed project documentation and compliance with ethical standards.

**Tools:** biosignalsplux, OpenSignals, Python, scikit-learn, NumPy
