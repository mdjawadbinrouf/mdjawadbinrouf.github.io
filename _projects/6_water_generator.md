Product design and development project, February 2023.

Designed and deployed an atmospheric water generator to address water scarcity in coastal areas, extracting moisture from air by dehumidification and applying multi-stage filtration.

**Tools:** SOLIDWORKS, ANSYS Fluent, Microsoft PowerPoint
