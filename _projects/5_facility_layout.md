Facility layout design project, May 2023.

Analyzed and redesigned the layout of the AUST Machine Shop laboratory to address spatial inefficiencies, workflow bottlenecks and safety hazards, optimizing equipment placement, space use and operational efficiency.

**Tools:** SketchUp, SOLIDWORKS, Microsoft PowerPoint
