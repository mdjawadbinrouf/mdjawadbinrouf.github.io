Undergraduate thesis, September 2024: **Performance Evaluation of Machine Learning Algorithms for Multivariate Energy Forecasting: Towards Effective Energy Management in Bangladesh**. Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Mohammad Sarwar Morshed.

- Evaluated machine learning models for time-series forecasting of total energy generation, non-renewable generation, fuel costs and demand in Bangladesh.
- Focused on improving forecasts for developing power systems, to support policymakers and stakeholders with data-driven, sustainable energy planning.

This work led to a journal article in *Energy Nexus* (2026); see [publications](/publications/).

**Tools:** Python, Jupyter Notebook, scikit-learn, Pandas, NumPy
