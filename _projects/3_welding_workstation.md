Workstation design project, July 2023.

Designed and proposed ergonomic enhancements to the AUST welding workstation, including improved furniture, lighting, noise control, ventilation and tool storage, to reduce operator strain and increase efficiency.

**Tools:** SOLIDWORKS, Microsoft PowerPoint
