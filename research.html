Research

Physiological workload in garment work

Research Assistant, AUST, April 2024 to June 2025. Principal Investigator: Mr. Shah Murtoza Morshed.

On the project Enhancing Occupational Safety and Ergonomics in Bangladesh's Ready-Made Garments Industry: Multisensory Workload Analysis with Machine Learning, I visited RMG factories, collected ECG, EMG, EDA and EEG signals from workers during garment-packaging tasks with biosignalsplux and OpenSignals, extracted features, and helped build machine learning models that classify physiological workload, tested with leave-one-subject-out cross-validation.

Machine learning for energy forecasting

Undergraduate thesis, 2024. Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Mohammad Sarwar Morshed.

Evaluated machine learning models for forecasting Bangladesh's total and non-renewable energy generation, fuel costs and demand, to support data-driven energy planning. This work led to a paper in Energy Nexus.

Forecasting and decision analytics

Ensemble learning for rice and jute supply chain forecasting, and fuzzy multi-criteria methods for sustainability decisions such as end-of-life vehicle management and renewable energy adoption. See publications.
