About me

I am a Lecturer in Industrial and Production Engineering at Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology in Dhaka. My research uses machine learning and decision analytics for energy management: forecasting generation, demand and fuel cost, studying what drives renewable energy adoption, and designing management systems for sustainable industry in emerging economies.

I graduated first of 87 students in my batch in 2025 and received the Khan Bahadur Ahsanullah Gold Medal. Before joining the faculty I worked as a research assistant on occupational ergonomics in Bangladesh's garment industry.
